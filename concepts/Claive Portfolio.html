About

Programmer who designs, designer who programs

I work across gameplay programming, game design and the business side of an indie studio. Most days that means writing movement and vehicle code in Unity, then playtesting it until it feels right.

I like cozy, warm and minimal design, and I lean on shaders to give small projects a strong look: outlines, CRT screens, acrylic paint and watercolor styles. I also enjoy game jams, where a three-day limit forces the core idea to show up fast. Away from the screen I take pictures with a Canon PowerShot A430.
